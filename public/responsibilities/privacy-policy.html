Privacy Policy

Responsibilities · Preview draft

Responsibilities stores the tasks you create, their schedules, reminder settings, completion history, and app preferences on your device. The app uses this information to show your tasks and history and schedule reminders. It does not require an account or sync your responsibilities to a server. Depending on your device settings, Apple may include app data in device backups.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, iOS schedules and delivers reminders on your device. A reminder can show the name of a responsibility. You can change how notifications appear or turn them off in iOS Settings.

Support and feedback

Help & Feedback opens an email draft. If you choose to send it, the developer receives your email address, message, and anything you include. The developer uses that information to respond and investigate your request. Avoid including private task details or screenshots you do not want to share.

Deleting your information

You can delete individual responsibilities from their detail screen. Deleting the app removes its local data from the device. Manage any existing device backups separately. For questions about information you sent by email, or to request its deletion, contact contact@osorioabel.dev.
