Using reminders

Reminders depend on your device, notification permissions, and iOS settings. They can be delayed, silenced, or missed. Use a separate reminder for critical deadlines.

Your responsibilities and history

The app keeps your responsibilities and history on your device. It does not provide an account that can restore them. Deleting the app removes its local data. If you rely on a device backup, check your backup settings before changing or resetting your device.
